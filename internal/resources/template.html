📚 Technical Analysis Indicators Explained

💰 Price Information

Current Price: Latest closing price of the stock

Price Change: Daily percentage change from previous close

Support Level: Price level where buying interest typically emerges (potential buying opportunity)

Resistance Level: Price level where selling pressure typically occurs (potential selling opportunity)

📈 Momentum Indicators

RSI (Relative Strength Index): Measures if stock is overbought (>70) or oversold (<30). Range: 0-100

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): Shows momentum changes. Positive = bullish, Negative = bearish

Stochastic %K: Compares closing price to price range over 14 periods. >80 = overbought, <20 = oversold

📊 Moving Averages

MA-5: 5-day moving average - Short-term trend indicator

MA-10: 10-day moving average - Very short-term trend

MA-20: 20-day moving average - Short to medium-term trend

MA-50: 50-day moving average - Medium-term trend

MA-200: 200-day moving average - Long-term trend (major support/resistance)

When price > MA = bullish trend, When price < MA = bearish trend

🎯 Bollinger Bands

BB Upper: Upper boundary of price volatility (2 standard deviations above MA-20)

BB Lower: Lower boundary of price volatility (2 standard deviations below MA-20)

Price near upper band = potentially overbought

Price near lower band = potentially oversold

Bands contracting = low volatility, Bands expanding = high volatility

🎯 Trading Signals

RSI Signal: HOLD (30-70), BUY (<30 oversold), SELL (>70 overbought)

MACD Signal: BUY when MACD > Signal line, SELL when MACD < Signal line

MA Trend Signal: BUY when price > short MA > long MA, SELL when opposite

Bollinger Signal: BUY near lower band, SELL near upper band

Overall Recommendation: Combined signal from all indicators

💡 How to Use This Analysis

BUY Signals: Multiple indicators showing bullish momentum

SELL Signals: Multiple indicators showing bearish momentum

HOLD Signals: Mixed signals or neutral territory

Risk Management: Use support/resistance levels for stop-losses

Confirmation: Wait for multiple indicators to agree before trading
